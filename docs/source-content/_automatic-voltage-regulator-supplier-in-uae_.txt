What Is an Automatic Voltage Regulator? An Automatic Voltage Regulator is an electronic control unit that automatically stabilizes voltage by compensating for overvoltage, undervoltage, and load-related fluctuations. Voltage instability can occur due to changing loads, grid variations, or environmental conditions, and without proper regulation, this can lead to equipment damage, reduced efficiency, and unexpected downtime.
