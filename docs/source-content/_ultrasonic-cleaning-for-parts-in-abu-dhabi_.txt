What is Ultrasonic Cleaning? Ultrasonic cleaning uses high-frequency sound waves in a specialized cleaning solution to create microscopic bubbles that safely remove contaminants even from tight spaces that traditional methods can’t reach. It’s eco-friendly, non-abrasive, and ideal for precision components where mechanical scrubbing or harsh chemicals could cause damage.
