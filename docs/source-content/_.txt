Pistons & Piston Rings At JRS , we supply a robust range of pistons and piston rings designed for slow, medium, and high-speed marine engines. Pistons Our pistons are engineered for strength, durability, and efficient heat resistance. Manufactured using high-grade materials such as lamellar and nodular graphite cast iron, they are coated with advanced finishes—Chromium Ceramic, metallic plasma spray, or ceramic coatings—to reduce wear and enhance corrosion resistance. These coatings ensure optimal lubrication and extend engine life by forming a fine oil film between the liner and the piston ring. Piston Rings We offer precision piston rings suitable for four-stroke engines with bore diameters up to 250 mm. Designed for high performance, our piston rings meet the highest standards for quality, sealing efficiency, and application versatility, helping to maintain peak engine performance and fuel efficiency. Liners & Anti Polishing Rings At JRS , we partner with experienced manufacturers specializing in high-performance cylinder liners and anti-polishing rings for diesel and gas engines used in marine and power generation applications. Our liners are centrifugally cast using premium-grade cast iron, ranging from 150 mm to 500 mm in diameter, and are compatible with a wide range of engine models. To ensure superior durability and abrasion resistance, our liners are made from alloyed lamellar graphite cast iron—engineered to perform under the toughest conditions. Most liners are pre-honed for easy installation and optimal oil film formation. Upon request, additional treatments such as annealing, chromium or nickel plating, and roller burnishing can be arranged to suit specific operational requirements. All cylinder liners supplied by JRS meet stringent industry standards and are certified by major classification societies, assuring quality, reliability, and long service life.
