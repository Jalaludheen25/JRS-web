REPLACEMENT ENGINE SPARE PARTS

We offer a comprehensive range of premium engine spare parts for all major marine engines. Sourced from trusted OEMs and leading manufacturers, our parts are engineered for superior performance, reliability, and long-lasting durability in demanding marine environments.

Products We Serve

Engine Bearings

We provide high-quality engine bearings designed to withstand the rigorous demands of marine diesel engines. Our range includes main, thrust, and connecting rod bearings—manufactured to OEM specifications for superior durability, load capacity, and smooth engine operation under extreme conditions.

Cylinder Heads & Components

At JRS, we offer a comprehensive selection of high-quality cylinder head components engineered to meet the demands of modern marine engines—both 2-stroke and 4-stroke. Our focus is on durability, performance, and long-term value for our clients operating in harsh marine environments.

Valve Stems
We supply valve stems compatible with a wide range of engine models. Built for endurance in high-temperature combustion zones, our offerings include Nimonic exhaust valves and stems with thermal barrier coatings to enhance corrosion resistance and extend service life.

Valve Seats
Our valve seats are available in both cooled and uncooled versions, featuring hardened surfaces and acidic-corrosion resistance. Manufactured using advanced techniques, these components are designed to prevent coolant leaks and ensure reliable performance.

Valve Rotators
JRS offers precision-engineered valve rotators, including Ball and Spring types as well as advanced Turnomats. These components are crafted in Europe to exact tolerances and ensure even heat distribution across the valve head—minimizing localized stress and extending the life of critical engine parts.

We also supply a complete range of supporting cylinder head components, including:

  • Valve Springs

  • Valve Guides

  • Collets

  • Cylinder Head Gaskets & O-Rings

Fuel Injection Systems

We supply precision-engineered fuel injection systems and components for marine diesel engines, ensuring optimal combustion, fuel efficiency, and engine performance. Our range includes injectors, pumps, nozzles, and complete system assemblies—sourced from trusted OEMs and built for durability in demanding marine conditions.

Pistons & Piston Rings

At JRS, we supply a robust range of pistons and piston rings designed for slow, medium, and high-speed marine engines. Sourced from leading European manufacturers with decades of technical expertise, our components are built to perform in demanding operating conditions.

Pistons
Our pistons are engineered for strength, durability, and efficient heat resistance. Manufactured using high-grade materials such as lamellar and nodular graphite cast iron, they are coated with advanced finishes—Chromium Ceramic, metallic plasma spray, or ceramic coatings—to reduce wear and enhance corrosion resistance. These coatings ensure optimal lubrication and extend engine life by forming a fine oil film between the liner and the piston ring.

Piston Rings
We offer precision piston rings suitable for four-stroke engines with bore diameters up to 250 mm. Designed for high performance, our piston rings meet the highest standards for quality, sealing efficiency, and application versatility, helping to maintain peak engine performance and fuel efficiency.

Liners & Anti Polishing Rings

At JRS, we partner with experienced manufacturers specializing in high-performance cylinder liners and anti-polishing rings for diesel and gas engines used in marine and power generation applications. Our liners are centrifugally cast using premium-grade cast iron, ranging from 150 mm to 500 mm in diameter, and are compatible with a wide range of engine models.

To ensure superior durability and abrasion resistance, our liners are made from alloyed lamellar graphite cast iron—engineered to perform under the toughest conditions. Most liners are pre-honed for easy installation and optimal oil film formation. Upon request, additional treatments such as annealing, chromium or nickel plating, and roller burnishing can be arranged to suit specific operational requirements.

All cylinder liners supplied by JRS meet stringent industry standards and are certified by major classification societies, assuring quality, reliability, and long service life.

Filters

At JRS, we supply a comprehensive range of high-quality marine filters designed to protect critical engine and auxiliary systems from contaminants. Our offerings include oil filters, fuel filters, air filters, and water separators—engineered for maximum filtration efficiency and durability.

Whether for routine maintenance or urgent replacements, our filters meet OEM standards and are compatible with a wide variety of marine engine models. Each unit is built to ensure optimal system performance, reduced wear, and extended equipment life—even under demanding marine conditions.

Turbochargers & Cartridges

JRS supplies high-quality spare parts for a wide range of marine turbochargers, supporting efficient engine performance and reduced downtime at sea. We specialize in OEM components, sourced from trusted manufacturers.

We offer spare parts compatible with leading turbocharger brands, including:

✔ MAN

Our product range includes critical components such as casings, rotors, nozzle rings, labyrinth seals, and bearing assemblies—all engineered for durability and optimal performance in demanding marine conditions.

Coolers & Heat Exchangers

JRS delivers reliable coolers and heat exchangers designed for efficient thermal management in marine applications. Our range includes charge air, lube oil, freshwater, and seawater coolers—available as complete units or spare parts like tube stacks, gaskets, and seals. Built for durability and corrosion resistance, all components ensure optimal performance at sea.

Our Services

Engine Overhauls

At JRS, our skilled engineers and technicians deliver comprehensive engine overhaul solutions with a focus on safety, precision, and global responsiveness.

Our capabilities include:

  • Complete overhaul of cylinder heads

  • Inspection and overhaul of pistons & connecting rods

  • Removal, refitting, and honing/deglazing of liners

  • Main & thrust bearing inspections

  • Overhaul of fuel pumps and injectors

  • Camshaft and bush inspections and replacements

  • Crankshaft deflection checks (pre & post overhaul)

  • Cleaning and overhaul of charge air and lube oil coolers

  • Overhaul of LO and FW pumps

  • Engine reassembly and load testing

  • Renewal of crankshaft and entablature

  • Line bore checks, line boring & laser alignment

We ensure reliable performance restoration through high-quality workmanship and timely service worldwide.

Turbocharger Overhauls

JRS offers expert turbocharger overhaul services, backed by a skilled team experienced in repairing and refurbishing critical rotating equipment. Our service center is equipped to handle all major turbocharger models with precision and efficiency.

Our services include:

  • Rotor balancing and re-blading

  • Thermal balancing

  • Replacement of partition wall sealing strips

We ensure reliable performance restoration and extended service life for your turbocharger systems.
